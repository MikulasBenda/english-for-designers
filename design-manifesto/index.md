# Design Manifesto 
## The Beginning 

Id like to think that the person standing here today, meaning my little old self, is a combination of a few important components. Components such as Childhood, Parents and Family influences, Important people through my life and something that doesn't have any rational explanation.
 
I was born in 2000 to a middle class family that despite some issues (which is of course in every family) loved me very much. Not much to say because I don’t really remember my pesonality changing developing moments that I could talk about, altho I have some ideas. I was as everybody else very curious, I liked to play with my toys or my friends, nothing out of ordinary.
My first dramtic change to my personalyty and overall developmnet was the death om my mother when I was 7 (ill get back to that) and because of that I guess I became from the center of attention seeking to a very timid in the back of the classroom type but I guess my class was a big part of it too, lets just say my peers were not the nicest kisds (some of them of course), not to say I was bullied but they were really rude and agresive and I didn’t want nothing to do with them so I stood by and held back.
Then my father had the idea of moving me to another school and I think that was a really good call, because of it I finnally found some friends I could hang out with and develop as a person. Those were the years, but as everyting in this world it had to end at some point and I had to make a decition of what I want to be. I always liked the idea of a cook so when the time to choose my high school came, that was my first choice.. but my dad also noticed that I liked to draw and lets be honest, culinary carrier is really tough and only a few make it their dream carrier so he sugested I should try a graphic design school, so I visited both and with some valid points from my dad I chose graphic design, and thank god. Graphic design it was witch is weird when I look back at it, because it didn’t really make any sence, last time I was “creative” was when I was like.. 3-4 years old and then I stoped so I didn’t have any real motivation or skills to pursue a creative carrier. But.. but, then I guess something clicked, when it came to drawing/painting lessons it made me realize “hey this is kinda fun” I honed my skills and life went on and I really got into painting and drawing I would even say for a 16 year old that didn’t have any real art background (apart from my grandpa) I wasnt half bad. But then life got in the way and I fell in love.. typical. I stoped creating art. I was so focused on this girl that I didn’t have any time to create and for some time I even thougt I had lost it, but alas it wasn't so and now I can finally start this “resume”. Also it feels like the more I wright these words the more I realize how crazy it is that any “worth while” skills that could actualy make me an interesting person to get me some cool job is the last year and a half.
I have started my creative journey year and a half ago, as I said I am making art since my 16 and working in graphic design from my 19. Its a curse and a blessing at the same time to create graphic design as “art”, thats how I take it anyways, I cant help it. I would like to be more focused and  in line with graphic designer flow but the first half im putting into the work is my artistic side, and the second a 23 year old that knows only the basics of the programs im working with. On the bright side I feel motivated every time I discover some new and easier way to do a particular task. I also like that im not like my dad in this, that is that I don’t need to be the so called leader of the group to find my meaning it the work, Im a team player and I would love to one day work with a group of people that together we coul all bring something to the table. I am also becoming more organised then ever, im wrigting everything down to my callendar so I guess people change for the better too :D.
