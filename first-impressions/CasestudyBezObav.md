# Case Study: Visual Identity for Bez Obav
## Bachelor’s Graphic Design Project

## Project Overview
For my bachelor’s assignment, I created a full visual identity and communication system for Bez Obav – a fictional Czech charity organization inspired by real ones like Bez Tíže and Proxima Sociale. The organization is designed to support children and young adults facing difficult mental, family, or social situations, offering counseling, financial help, and a safe space to grow.

## Goals
My aim was not only to build a complete visual identity for a socially impactful organization but also to fully dive into the process of developing a brand from scratch. I wanted to understand how design can support trust, approachability, and care in an emotionally sensitive context.

## Process & Challenges
The design process was long and layered. I started with research, including an interview with a team member from one of the real organizations. I explored different directions, both digitally and by hand — sketching, making posters, and experimenting with textures and forms.

I went through three major visual styles:

- The first was too artsy and abstract, failing to communicate clarity and trust.

- The second leaned too corporate – clean, but it felt more like a bank than a charity.

- The third, which became the final, evolved through several refinements and ended up capturing the    balance of warmth, structure, and professionalism.


## Final Output
The final visual identity includes:

A logo and color palette

Typography system

Poster series (digital and handmade)

Social media templates

Brand tone of voice and communication examples

Applications for print and digital use


## Reflection
This project pushed me creatively and strategically. It helped me understand how important it is to match design with emotion, message, and audience – especially in the nonprofit space. I learned that failure in early phases often leads to stronger outcomes, and that working by hand can reveal new ideas that the computer misses.
