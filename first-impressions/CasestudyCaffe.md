
# Case Study: Visual Identity for Café Letka
Second-Year Project — Graphic Design Bachelor


## Project Overview

This project marked a personal turning point for me — my first assignment after rejoining my second year due to health issues. It was more than just a school task; it was a way to reintroduce myself as a designer. The challenge? Redesign the visual identity for Café Letka, a charming spot near Prague’s Letná Park, without confusing it with another nearby café that had already adopted the style from our pilot brief.

## The Goal

Our goal was to develop a fresh, authentic visual identity that matched Letka’s unique personality — a mix of old-school charm and modern hipster energy — without drawing unintentional connections to other cafés in the area. The name "Letka" connects to Letná Park, not the other café, and the identity had to reflect that.

## Teamwork & Collaboration

This was also a team project. I worked with a classmate named Karel. Let’s just say — we were very different. After some creative tension and strong opinions, we agreed to work separately while still exchanging feedback. It ended up being the best of both worlds: collaboration without conflict. And yes, we’re still friends.

## Creative Process

At first, I leaned into the obvious — the iconic Letná metronome, a symbol of independence — but it felt too forced. I shifted to something more fitting: a minimalist black-and-white outline of a coffee cup, softened and energized by colorful abstract shapes in the background. This contrast captured the vibe of Café Letka’s cozy interiors and modern marketing feel — simple, elegant, and playful.

## Final Design & Outcome
The final design features:

A clean line-art coffee cup logo

"Café Letka" hand-placed at the center

A burst of colorful organic shapes in the background for personality

A system that works across posters, merch, and digital spaces


## Reflection
To my surprise, the presentation was one of my best yet. I was confident, relaxed, and myself — even got some laughs. Out of 10–12 teams, our project earned third place. But more importantly, this was a win for me personally: I returned, I created, I connected. Café Letka helped me find my rhythm again.
